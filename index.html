The Problem

Changes Between Appointments Are Hard to Spot.

Older adults and people with long-term conditions may juggle several devices and apps, and a single reading means little without knowing what is normal for that person.

Sustained Changes Go Unnoticed

Single readings miss gradual changes in heart rate, HRV, sleep and activity that build up over several days.

Families Worry Between Visits

Relatives may worry about older family members living independently, and today's apps rarely give them a simple, consent-based view.

Isolated Data & Anxious Families

Most smartwatch and fitness apps show separate metrics, not changes against your own long-term baseline, and data sits in different apps.

Our Solution

Everwell: Your Signals, One Clear View.

Everwell connects to compatible wearables you may already own, and to a compatible continuous glucose monitor (CGM) where available. Over about four weeks it builds a personal baseline, then explains changes in plain language.

  • ✓
    Learns Your Personal Baseline: Rather than relying on population averages, Everwell builds a 4-week baseline from your own readings.
  • ✓
    Highlights Sustained Changes: Flags changes that last several days, not one-off readings. Alerts are not diagnoses.
  • ✓
    Consent-Based Sharing: You choose what to share, and with whom: family, caregivers or your clinician.

Key Benefits

What Everwell Aims to Offer

Designed for adults aged about 55+ and people managing long-term conditions, starting in Italy.

Clearer Conversations With Your Clinician

Share a simple summary of sustained changes, so follow-up decisions stay with healthcare professionals.

Your Personal Baseline

Everwell compares new readings with your own usual pattern, not population averages, and shows the context behind each change.

Reassurance for Distant Families

Optional, consent-based updates keep family members informed without constant calls.

Privacy by Design

Minimal data, consent-based sharing you can withdraw at any time, and a GDPR assessment before any pilot. We do not plan to sell health data.

Business Model (Proposed)

How Everwell Could Work as a Business

Everything here is a proposal to test. We will choose one main route for the MVP rather than launching every channel at once.

Revenue Model

A one-time sale of a selected compatible wearable or bundle, plus a monthly or annual app subscription for advanced trends, reports and sharing. Provider agreements, where a care organisation pays for a group of users, may follow later. Pricing will be tested in interviews and the pilot.

Channels & Partners

Individual buyers, including people who already own a compatible device, introduced by pharmacies, diabetes clinics and healthcare professionals. Home-care and remote-care providers are a possible later channel. We would also need wearable and CGM data-access partners.

Italy First

Italy is our proposed launch market because its ageing population makes at-home support for older adults a relevant challenge to investigate. Other European markets would come only after the MVP shows user value, technical reliability and a viable business model.

Privacy, Regulation & Impact

We plan to minimise data, control access and explain consent clearly, assess whether a Data Protection Impact Assessment is needed, and get specialist regulatory advice before launch. Everwell aims to contribute to UN SDG 3 (Good Health and Well-being); this impact will be evaluated, not assumed.
