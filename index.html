The Problem

Chronic Care Today Is Reactive, Fragmented, and Costly.

For older adults living with conditions like heart failure or diabetes, physiological changes develop gradually over days—often going undetected until an emergency room visit becomes unavoidable.

Sustained Changes Go Unnoticed

Single spot-check readings fail to capture multi-day trends. Subclinical fluid retention, heart rate variability dips, and glucose spikes occur without obvious early symptoms.

Stressful & Unnecessary Clinic Visits

Elderly patients and family caregivers endure repeated, exhausting clinic commutes just to confirm basic stability, overburdening cardiology departments and regional healthcare systems.

Isolated Data & Anxious Families

Standard fitness trackers cater to young athletes and trigger false alarms, leaving distant family members and telemedicine clinicians without actionable, trusted clinical context.

Our Solution

Everwell: Comprehensive At-Home Health Intelligence.

Everwell bridges daily living with clinical peace of mind. By combining a comfortable multi-sensor wearable with seamless Continuous Glucose Monitor (CGM) integration and a secure AI cloud, Everwell continuously establishes each user's unique physiological baseline.

  • ✓
    Learns Your Unique Baseline: Rather than relying on generic population averages, Everwell builds a 30-day baseline tailored to your individual health profile.
  • ✓
    Detects Sustained Patterns: Distinguishes temporary exertion from meaningful physiological shifts in heart rate, HRV, nocturnal oxygen, and skin temperature.
  • ✓
    Consent-Based Care Circle: Directly connects users with family members, home-care nurses, and telemedicine providers for collaborative reassurance.

How It Works

Three Simple Steps to Continuous Reassurance

Designed for ease of use by seniors—no technical setup or complicated menus required.

01

Wear & Seamlessly Sync

Put on the comfortable, lightweight Everwell wristband. It automatically tracks heart rate, HRV, SpO₂, and skin temperature, and wirelessly syncs with your CGM sensor.

Zero manual calibration needed
02

AI Learns Your Baseline

Our intelligent cloud software analyzes your day-and-night health rhythms over time, establishing an accurate personal baseline of your everyday physiology.

Adaptive machine learning
03

Receive Insights & Share

View your clean, high-contrast mobile dashboard. If sustained trends indicate potential health risks, an early alert is gently flagged to you and your chosen care partners.
